## VEDAL R1, R2 — neonatal resuscitation system

Open resuscitation systems (ORS): neonatal equipment for intensive care,
emergency assistance and nursing of infants. They provide open 360-degree access
to the child for surgical, diagnostic and resuscitation procedures, maintain
temperature through overhead radiant heating and monitor physiological
parameters.

Key features:

- **Radiant heating** — an infrared heater regulates temperature in manual or
  servo-controlled mode.
- **Constant access** — emergency manipulations, surgery, phototherapy and
  ventilation without moving the patient.
- **Monitoring** — built-in scales, SpO2, vital function control.
- **Resuscitation functions** — oxygen therapy, CPAP therapy, aspiration.

How R2 differs from R1: an LCD display, an accessory drawer and muting the alarm
with a hand wave — R2 only. Phototherapy, an aspirator and a respiratory support
unit are available as options for R2 only.

Dimensions and weight: R1 — 1185×740×1840 mm, 87.5 ± 2.0 kg; R2 —
1285×735×1850 mm, 113 ± 2 kg.

## VEDAL A-2000 — transformable incubator

A neonatal incubator that transforms into an open resuscitation system. It
combines the functionality of a closed incubator and an ORS; switching between
modes is done by electromechanical drives.

Advantages over a closed incubator:

- **Lower risk and stress** — a newborn in critical condition does not have to be
  moved between an incubator and a resuscitation system.
- **Continuity of care** — all modes are controlled by a single device and
  parameters stabilize automatically.
- **Versatility** — one device replaces two.
- **Conditions for staff** — convenient access to the patient in every mode.

Modes: "Incubator" — a closed environment with temperature and humidity control
for premature infants; "Heater" — even radiant heating from above, working as an
ORS with APGAR and CPR timers, an infusion stand, phototherapy and scales
(options).

Dimensions 1157×720×2290 mm, weight 160 ± 5 kg. Monitoring channels: pulse
oximetry, ECG, respiration, NIBP, capnometry. A 1920×1080 camera is built in,
with a USB-A interface.

## VEDAL Т-100 — medical thermoregulation system

For neonatology, resuscitation and neonatal intensive care units. It circulates
heated or cooled water through a thermoregulating blanket on the newborn's body
and regulates the water temperature according to the patient's body temperature.

The indications for use are listed in the datasheet — these are the
manufacturer's clinical formulations. They may be carried to the website only in
the exact form given there, and only after it is confirmed that they may be
published.

The liquid temperature range is 12.0–39.0 °C, the cooling rate 0.7 °C/min, the
heating rate 1.0 °C/min. Blankets come in two sizes: 580×390 mm and 425×275 mm.
