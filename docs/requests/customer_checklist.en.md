# What we need from the customer to close out the project

[Русский](customer_checklist.md) · **English**

## In short

The portal is technically ready: the website, the product catalog, request
intake, the account area and the Vedalina assistant all work. The only thing
standing between that and showing it in full is what is listed below —
materials, permission to publish certain things, access to a few accounts,
and a handful of decisions only the customer can make.

The list closes out sixteen of our internal tasks. Here they are gathered
into one table that can be worked through in an hour, instead of reading each
task on its own.

For every item: what to send or decide, what it unlocks on the site, and the
task number in our tracker, in case details are ever needed.

## Materials

### 1. Product photos and video

- **What is needed.** Final photos and video of the products, manufacturing,
  the office, the exhibition stand and the team — the customer is updating
  these through their own contractor, and we need the finished set.
- **Why.** Without them, the product cards and site sections keep provisional
  or empty visuals.
- **Task number.** #34.

### 2. Final texts and specifications for the four products

- **What is needed.** Approved descriptions, intended use, key advantages and
  the delivery set for `VEDAL A-2000`, `VEDAL R1`, `VEDAL R2`, `VEDAL T-100`.
- **Why.** Unlocks the product cards, which currently read "awaiting
  clarification," and gives the Vedalina assistant accurate text to answer
  visitors with, instead of a draft.
- **Task number.** #35, #38.

### 3. Product catalog as a single file

- **What is needed.** Sign-off on the combined catalog text for the four
  products (datasheets for them already exist) — no prices, that was already
  decided earlier.
- **Why.** The "Documents" page will get a real downloadable file instead of
  a request form.
- **Task number.** #75.

### 4. News items to publish first

- **What is needed.** Text, photos and permission to publish for the news
  items that should appear on the site first — for example, about
  participation in trade shows.
- **Why.** The "News" section on the site is built but currently empty.
- **Task number.** #35.

## Permissions

### 5. Registration and certification status

- **What is needed.** For each product, either an approved wording for its
  registration, certification and licensing status, or an explicit decision
  not to publish that line at all.
- **Why.** The product pages currently show the word "awaiting
  clarification." We cannot replace it with plausible-sounding wording
  ourselves — it is a claim about a medical device's regulatory clearance,
  not a copywriting choice.
- **Task number.** #72, #35.

### 6. Document listing for public access

- **What is needed.** The list of documents that may be shown on the site
  publicly, and which section each one belongs to (the site currently has
  four: "Licensing," "Quality System," "Technical Documentation," "Commercial
  Materials").
- **Why.** Decides what actually appears on the "Documents" page and what
  stays internal only.
- **Task number.** #35.

## Access and account details

### 7. Domain settings

- **What is needed.** Access to the `vedal-med.ru` domain settings (or to
  whoever manages them) so the site can be pointed at our server. We will not
  touch the domain's mail settings without separate agreement.
- **Why.** Without this the site stays on a temporary technical address and
  cannot get a secure connection (the padlock icon in the browser).
- **Task number.** #36.

### 8. Mailbox for request notifications

- **What is needed.** A working mailbox and access to send from it (address
  and password — the password is entered by the mailbox owner, not by us).
- **Why.** Right now an email about a new website request is marked as sent,
  but it never actually goes anywhere: no mailbox is connected.
- **Task number.** #46.

### 9. Social media links

- **What is needed.** Account addresses for Telegram, WhatsApp and VK — or a
  decision to remove those buttons from the site entirely.
- **Why.** The buttons exist on the site right now but lead nowhere.
- **Task number.** #69.

### 10. Correct company registration code and a details check

- **What is needed.** The correct KPP code for the company (the site
  currently shows 540601001), and a check that the INN, KPP and address in
  the company details do not contradict each other.
- **Why.** The "Contacts" page needs to show accurate company details, not a
  guess.
- **Task number.** #68.

### 11. Staff contacts

- **What is needed.** For each staff member who should appear on the site:
  name, position, work email, phone (shared or personal), and what to
  contact them about. Separately, a decision on whether to publish personal
  phone numbers at all.
- **Why.** The "Contacts" page currently shows "awaiting clarification"
  instead of a staff list.
- **Task number.** #77.

### 12. Business hours

- **What is needed.** One of two options: change the hours shown on the site
  to 9:00 AM–5:30 PM, or keep 6:00 PM and set up a phone auto-responder for
  the last half hour (that is a phone system setting, done by the customer).
- **Why.** The site currently says "until 6:00 PM," and the phone is not
  always answered at that time — a visitor calling late in the day just
  hears ringing.
- **Task number.** #76.

## Decisions

### 13. Retention period for website requests

- **What is needed.** Confirmation of how long website requests should be
  kept (we proposed 3 years, but the decision is the customer's).
- **Why.** A request contains the visitor's personal data, and without a
  confirmed retention period we cannot set up automatic deletion.
- **Task number.** #47.

### 14. Yandex Metrica

- **What is needed.** Permission to install the Yandex Metrica counter on the
  public site and collect visit statistics.
- **Why.** Without permission, the counter and its consent banner will not
  go live.
- **Task number.** #53.

### 15. Site languages

- **What is needed.** Confirmation of whether English and Chinese are needed
  in the upcoming release, and translations of descriptions, specifications
  and documents (machine translation is not acceptable for medical wording).
- **Why.** Without translations, multiple languages cannot go live even once
  the technical capability itself is ready.
- **Task number.** #54.

### 16. Data exchange with 1C

- **What is needed.** The name of who is responsible for 1C on the customer's
  side, an agreed exchange method (files, a dedicated channel, or manual
  entry), which request fields are mandatory, and how errors and duplicate
  requests should be handled.
- **Why.** Without this, website requests cannot flow into 1C automatically.
- **Task number.** #39.

### 17. On-call coverage

- **What is needed.** Confirmation of whether an on-call schedule is needed
  (who is responsible for website inquiries on a given day), or whether the
  current support hours are enough.
- **Why.** Without an answer, the "Conversations" section in the account area
  keeps showing "awaiting clarification" instead of a schedule, and there is
  no one to hand a shift over to.
- **Task number.** #51.

## What happens without an answer

No pressure and no deadlines here — just an honest account of what happens
if an answer does not arrive:

- **Without materials**, product cards and news stay with provisional text
  and empty photo slots, and the buttons on the "Documents" page keep leading
  to a request form instead of a file.
- **Without permissions**, the product pages either keep showing "awaiting
  clarification" next to the registration status, or we remove that line
  entirely — we will not invent the wording.
- **Without access and account details**, the site has no domain of its own
  and no secure connection, request emails do not arrive, and the social
  media buttons and some contacts stay empty or inaccurate.
- **Without the decisions**, the corresponding features simply stay off:
  no automatic deletion of personal data, no visit counter, the site stays
  Russian-only, requests do not flow into 1C on their own, and the
  "Conversations" section keeps showing "awaiting clarification" for on-call
  coverage.

None of this blocks showing the portal itself — the affected part just stays
in its current, unfinished state until an answer comes in.
