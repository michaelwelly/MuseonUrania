## Short text to send

Nikolay Nikolaevich, good afternoon.

We are starting to prepare the presentation and the technical plan for developing
VEDAL: the website, the product catalog, media and documents, CRM, corporate IT
infrastructure and AI search over internal materials.

To assemble a working version of the presentation by Friday, 24 July 2026, please
send a link to a cloud folder or an archive with the materials listed below on
23–24 July 2026. Corrections and clarifications are best received by the end of
Friday, 24 July 2026, so that on Monday, 27 July 2026, we can finalize the
presentation, verify the facts and prepare the release for the stakeholder
showing on Tuesday, 28 July 2026.

## What we need

1. The current product catalog, including the Innoprom catalog and materials.
2. A confirmed list of products for the first version of the website and the
   presentation. As currently understood, about 10 products are expected.
3. For each product:
   - the exact name;
   - the category;
   - the intended use;
   - 3–5 key advantages;
   - the main technical specifications;
   - registration and certification status;
   - photos, renders, video;
   - which documents may be shared publicly.
4. The press release, talking points or any materials from Innoprom.
5. Photos and video of manufacturing, the office, the exhibition stand, the team
   and the equipment.
6. Certificates, registration certificates, ISO 13485 and other documents that
   may be used publicly.
7. Technical, service and manufacturing documentation for the internal repository
   and future AI search.
8. Separately mark the materials that must not be published:
   - R&D;
   - manufacturing;
   - service instructions;
   - commercial terms;
   - suppliers;
   - internal developments;
   - any sensitive documentation.
9. Brand materials:
   - the logo;
   - brand colors;
   - typefaces;
   - the rules for writing VEDAL / Ведал;
   - existing presentations or brochures, if any.
10. Contacts of the responsible people for:
    - products;
    - manufacturing;
    - sales;
    - service;
    - legal wording;
    - document publication.

## What we need confirmed

1. The primary domain: `vedal-med.ru` or another option.
2. Whether the current website must be preserved and extended, or a new version
   may be prepared.
3. Which languages are needed in the first release: Russian, English, Chinese.
   We propose Hindi as a separate stage.
4. Whether Yandex Metrica may be installed on the public website right away.
5. Which stack is preferred for the corporate infrastructure: Yandex, Google,
   Kontur or a hybrid.
6. Whether the CRM is needed in the first stage or as a second stage after the
   website.
7. Whether there are data residency constraints: Russia only, a specific cloud
   provider, a closed contour, VPN.
8. Which materials may be used in public SEO and which belong only in the
   internal knowledge base.
9. The Ural CCI membership mark: are we allowed to publish it on the site, and
   where is the source file kept. The mark belongs to the chamber, not to VEDAL,
   and handing the file over on 18 August is not by itself permission to publish
   it. The mark currently sits on the "About" page and links to `uralcci.com`.
   Without permission we take it down.
